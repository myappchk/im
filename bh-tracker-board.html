Incident Summary
Incident Summary
Resident was administered 50mg of Quetiapine instead of the prescribed 25mg during the evening medication pass.
Detailed Description
At approximately 8:45 PM during the evening medication pass, nursing staff administered 50mg of Quetiapine to the resident instead of the prescribed 25mg dose. The error was identified by the charge nurse during the post-administration medication reconciliation check. The resident had already swallowed the medication. Physician was notified immediately and a clinical assessment was initiated.
Sequence of Events
8:45 PMEvening medication pass initiated. Nursing staff prepared resident's medications.
8:52 PM50mg Quetiapine administered to resident. Prescribed dose was 25mg.
9:05 PMCharge nurse identified dosing discrepancy during post-administration reconciliation check.
9:08 PMAttending physician notified. Clinical assessment of resident initiated.
9:20 PMResident assessed — vitals stable, no adverse symptoms noted. Monitoring protocol activated.
Immediate Response
Other Actions Taken
Attending physician was notified immediately. Resident vitals were monitored continuously for two hours post-incident. Family was contacted and informed of the medication error. Incident was documented in the resident's medical record and pharmacy was notified.

Resident Assessment
Resident Behavior Observed
Resident appeared calm and cooperative. No signs of distress or adverse reaction noted immediately following administration.
Condition Before Incident
Resident was in stable condition prior to the medication pass. Vital signs within normal limits.
Condition After Incident
Resident remained stable. Increased sedation noted approximately 30 minutes post-administration. Vitals monitored and remained within acceptable range.
Contributing Factors
Similar medication packaging for 25mg and 50mg tablets, high census on unit, staff fatigue during late shift, insufficient double-check protocol adherence.
